ACME Corp Remote Work Policy

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 6-month probation may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.

2. Core Hours
Remote employees must be available between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time for meetings and collaboration.

3. Equipment and Security
Company laptops must be used for all work. A VPN connection is mandatory when accessing internal systems. Confidential documents must never be stored on personal devices.

4. Internet Allowance
Employees working remotely receive a monthly internet allowance of 1,000 INR, claimed through the expense process.
