ACME Corp Code of Conduct

1. Respectful Workplace
ACME has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination of any kind. Concerns can be raised with HR directly or reported anonymously through the Ethics Hotline, which is available 24 hours a day.

2. Gifts and Hospitality
Gifts from vendors or clients worth more than 2,000 INR must be declared to the Compliance team within 7 days. Cash gifts must always be refused.

3. Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment or financial interest in a vendor, client or competitor to their manager and HR.

4. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour lunch break.
