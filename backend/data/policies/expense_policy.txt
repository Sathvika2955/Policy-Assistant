ACME Corp Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Eligible Expenses
Business travel, client meals, accommodation, and approved software subscriptions are reimbursable. Personal expenses, fines, and alcohol are not reimbursable.

2. Submission Deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, with original receipts attached. Claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected.

3. Approval Limits
Claims up to 5,000 INR are approved by your direct manager. Claims between 5,000 and 25,000 INR require department head approval. Claims above 25,000 INR require Finance Director approval.

4. Daily Meal Allowance
During business travel, the meal allowance is 1,500 INR per day. Amounts above this limit require written justification.

5. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are paid within 10 working days via direct bank transfer.
