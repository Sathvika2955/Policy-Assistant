ACME Corp Business Travel Policy

1. Air Travel
Employees fly economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and requires director approval. All flights must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days before departure.

2. Hotel Accommodation
The hotel limit is 6,000 INR per night in metro cities and 4,000 INR per night in all other cities. Stays above the limit need prior approval from your manager.

3. Local Travel
Cab fares are reimbursed with receipts. If you use your personal vehicle for business travel, mileage is paid at 12 INR per km.

4. Travel Advance
You may request an advance of up to 50% of the estimated trip cost, submitted at least 5 days before travel. The advance is settled against your expense claim after the trip.
