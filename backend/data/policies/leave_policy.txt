ACME Corp Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year; any remaining days expire on March 31.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Paternity leave is 2 weeks of fully paid leave and must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

4. Applying for Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for annual leave. Your manager will approve or reject the request within 2 working days.
