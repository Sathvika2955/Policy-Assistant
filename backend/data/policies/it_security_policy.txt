ACME Corp IT Security Policy

1. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.

2. Lost or Stolen Devices
If a laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours so that the device can be remotely locked and wiped.

3. Phishing
Report any suspicious email using the Report Phishing button in your mail client. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.

4. Software and Devices
Only software approved by IT may be installed on company laptops. USB storage devices are disabled by default; exceptions require written approval from the IT security team.
